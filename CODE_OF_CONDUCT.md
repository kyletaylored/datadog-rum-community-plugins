# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume the person on the other side of the thread is trying to ship
something that works.

## Standards

Expected: direct, specific, technically-grounded feedback. Disagreement about a mapping, a schema
choice or an approach is the normal business of this project, and a well-argued objection is a
contribution.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, demeaning or discriminatory comments, publishing
anyone's private information, or sustained disruption.

One specific to this project: **never paste real customer data** — order payloads with real emails,
names, addresses or anything card-related — into an issue, a PR or a fixture. Redact first. A
redacted payload is still useful for debugging; a real one is a problem for someone who never agreed
to be in a public repository.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is representing the
project.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems to the maintainer at
[kyle.taylor@datadoghq.com](mailto:kyle.taylor@datadoghq.com). Reports are handled privately.

As maintainer I may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and PRs that conflict with this, and
will explain why when I do.
